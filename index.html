Our Story

WonderCraft began with a simple problem every parent knows — a restless child, a tablet within reach, and a wish for something better. We started hand-casting plaster shapes on a kitchen table, and today those same kits reach classrooms, birthday parties and homes across the country. Our promise hasn't changed: safe materials, honest pricing, and a little more creativity in every home.
